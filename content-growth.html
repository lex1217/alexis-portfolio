04 / Performance comparison

Compare patterns, then explain their limits

The original deck placed two detailed charts of Shorts and long-form organic views in sequence. Exact periods and chart values are held for publication review.

These are layout schematics, not campaign data or a claim about the size of any result. Approved values and matching reporting periods are required before charting performance.

What this shows The original before/during comparison examined how two YouTube formats moved over time.

Why it matters Side-by-side periods can reveal whether performance became more consistent, but they do not prove one tactic caused every change.

How I communicated it I paired matching chart formats with concise interpretation, allowing viewers to compare patterns before reading the conclusion.

06 / Synthesis

Tell the whole business story

The original conclusion connected retention, discovery, distribution, and amplification. This portfolio version replaces its agency sales call to action with the communication lesson.

  1. PackageMake a long episode easier to discover and navigate.
  2. RepurposeTurn strong moments into distinct audience entry points.
  3. ObserveCompare retention and reach with consistent definitions.
  4. AmplifyUse paid channels to extend content with organic traction.

What this shows How several tactics form one coherent growth narrative.

Why it matters Stakeholders need the relationship between decisions and outcomes, not a collection of isolated metrics.

How I communicated it I sequenced the study from overview to specific evidence and ended with a synthesis that a non-specialist could use.
