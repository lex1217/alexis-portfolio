About

I started my career in digital marketing, where I built a foundation in campaign strategy, performance analysis, client strategy, SEO, and digital advertising. Over time, I became increasingly interested in what was happening underneath the marketing work: where the data came from, how platforms connected, and how better systems could make the work more useful and scalable.

Today, I work as a Data Systems & Automation Specialist at Performance Digital, where my focus has expanded into marketing data infrastructure, reporting systems, APIs, automation, AI workflows, tracking, and marketing technology.

I enjoy working at the intersection of strategy and technology. I like understanding the business problem first, then determining how data, systems, automation, and AI can support a better solution.
