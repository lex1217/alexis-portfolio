The story so far

I began in digital marketing strategy and execution, working across campaign planning, paid media, performance analysis, SEO, and client strategy. I wanted to understand why campaigns performed the way they did, which drew me deeper into analytics and measurement.

Today, I’m a Data Systems & Automation Specialist at Performance Digital. I work across marketing data infrastructure, reporting systems, APIs, workflow automation, tracking, and AI-enabled tools that connect strategy with execution.

I became more technical because I kept running into marketing questions the existing setup could not answer. I wanted to know which actions showed real interest, understand why a campaign performed the way it did, and give teams information they could use. Learning the data and workflow side helped me answer those questions and fix what was getting in the way.
